Software engineer and machine learning engineer. Electrical and Computer Engineering at Princeton.

I take vague ideas and turn them into working products, fast. I've shipped full-stack apps, multi-agent systems, RAG pipelines, and a patient-safety app built in 48 hours at a hackathon. Behind the speed is rigor: my projects run on real backends with real deployments, and the AI systems come with eval harnesses, observability, and cost tracking, because a system you can't measure is one you can't trust.

For a research project in Princeton's Computer Science department, I developed SMARRT-PAL, a reasoning architecture for program-aided language models: roughly 5% higher accuracy while cutting LLM inference cost by about a third. You can read the write-up here. Outside of research I build at hackathons, from AI agents and software to 3D printing and hardware, and I build with Claude Code. Long term, I want to work on AI safety and alignment.
